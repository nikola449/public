# Novuk Home Brilliance

Create a modern, ultra-professional, mobile-responsive website for a high-end residential general contractor named "Novuk Home Projects", serving the Kitchener-Waterloo (KW), Cambridge, and Guelph regions in Ontario.

DESIGN &amp; BRANDING:
- Color Palette: Deep Navy Blue (#0B192C) primary, Slate Gray secondary, and Warm Metallic Gold (#D4AF37) accent highlights on dark or clean white cards.
- Hero Motto: "Your Home. Done Right."
- Tone: Premium, trustworthy, organized, and authoritative.

NAVIGATION &amp; LAYOUT:
- Top Header: Logo text "NOVUK Home Projects" with gold accent, navigation links (Fencing, Concrete &amp; Driveways, Painting, About, Contact), and a prominent gold CTA button "[Request a Yard Walkthrough]".

HOMEPAGE SECTIONS:
1. Hero Banner: Heavy background visual, headline "Your Home. Done Right.", subheadline "Kitchener-Waterloo's premier contractor for custom fencing, concrete driveways &amp; patios, and interior repaints." CTAs: "[Start Your Project Details]" and "[Book Yard Walkthrough]".
2. Trust &amp; Safety Bar: Highlight 4 badges — "Ontario One Call Utility Locates", "48" Deep Below-Frost Footings", "2-Year Structural Warranty", and "50/50 Shared Neighbor Billing".
3. Fencing Division (Broad &amp; Comprehensive): Highlight all fence materials and styles — Pressure-Treated Wood, Cedar, Vinyl/PVC, Chain Link, and Composite. Also feature Custom Gate Building, Post Hole Machine Augering &amp; Setting (48" deep), Fence Repairs, and Shared 50/50 Neighbor Property Line Installs.
4. Concrete &amp; Driveway Division: Feature Custom Concrete Driveways, Extensions &amp; Aprons, Patio Slabs, Hot Tub Pad Foundations, Footings, and Decorative Sealing.
5. Painting Division: Feature Year-Round Interior Residential Repaints, Trim/Door Refinishing, and Exterior Wood Staining.
6. Custom Project Intake Form (NO AUTOMATED PRICES):
   - Headline: "Submit Your Project Details for an On-Site Estimate"
   - Subtitle: "We evaluate every yard in person to ensure exact footings, slope management, and zero hidden fees."
   - Form Fields:
     * Full Name &amp; Phone Number (preferred contact: Text or Call)
     * Service Address (Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Guelph)
     * Service Required (Fencing, Concrete Driveway/Patio, Interior Painting, Multi-Service)
     * Fencing Options (Dropdown: Wood, Cedar, Vinyl, Chain Link, Composite, Repair/Post Setting)
     * Property / Yard Details (Scope size, sloped terrain check, narrow gate access)
     * Shared Neighbor Status (Checkbox/Radio: "Splitting cost 50/50 with neighbor?")
     * File Upload box for yard/driveway photos
     * Submit button: "[Submit Details for Walkthrough]"
   - Submission Message: "Thank you! Our team will review your details and contact you within 24 hours to schedule your on-site walkthrough."
7. Footer: Service area listing (Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Guelph), copyright, and contact links.
